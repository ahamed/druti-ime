Druti @VERSION@
Type Bengali phonetically in any app on your Mac.
https://github.com/ahamed/druti-ime

INSTALL

1. Double-click Druti. It copies itself into your Input Methods folder
   (~/Library/Input Methods), turns itself on, and tells you when it's done.
2. If macOS says it can't verify Druti: open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Druti, and open Druti
   again. macOS asks once, because Druti isn't notarized by Apple.
3. Choose Druti in the input menu in the menu bar, or press Control-Space
   (or the globe key) to switch to it.

Keep ABC or U.S. enabled as well. macOS types passwords with it, and you can
switch to it if Druti ever misbehaves.

You can eject this disk image and delete it after installing.

UPDATE

Download the newer DMG and double-click Druti again. It replaces the old
version and keeps your settings.

UNINSTALL

Choose "Uninstall Druti..." from Druti's input menu. It removes Druti from
your input sources and moves it to the Trash.

REQUIREMENTS

macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
